## Capstone: personal interactive website
Inspired by [felixrieseberg.com](https://felixrieseberg.com/?room=1): an interactive 3D scene (a desk at night by a rainy window, a TV showing his name, a VCR, labelled video tapes) where the tapes hold his work, projects, talks and posts. Devices that can't run it smoothly get a static version with the same content.

Pulls together several modules:
- Modules 6–7 (Claude Code: building apps): the site itself.
- Modules 9–11 (personal agent): could live in the site as a "talk to me" feature.
- Modules 15–16 (Claude Design): the look and feel of the scene.
- Content: the projects from this repo become the "tapes".
